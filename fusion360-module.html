Fusion 360 Module

A 7-day plan for learning Fusion 360 — moving from the interface and basic sketching to full 3D modeling, assemblies, and a final project.

DAY01
Getting Started
Introduction & Interface
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 1

Day 1 introduces Fusion 360 as a combined CAD, CAM, and CAE tool, and gets you comfortable moving around its workspace before you draw your first sketch.

  • What is Fusion 360 (CAD, CAM, CAE basics)
  • Workspace overview (Design, Solid, Surface)
  • Navigation (zoom, pan, orbit)
  • Create your first sketch
📘 What I Learned

I learned that Fusion 360 combines CAD, CAM and CAE in one tool, and got comfortable navigating the workspace — zooming, panning and orbiting around a model.

DAY02
Sketching
Sketching (2D Basics)
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 2

Day 2 focuses on 2D sketching fundamentals — the sketch tools and constraints that keep every design fully defined and free of errors.

  • Sketch tools: Line, Circle, Arc, Rectangle
  • Constraints: Coincident, Parallel, Perpendicular
  • Dimensions
📘 What I Learned

I learned the core 2D sketch tools and how constraints like coincident, parallel and perpendicular keep a sketch fully defined and free of errors.

DAY03
Sketching
Advanced Sketch Practice
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 3

Day 3 builds on the basics with tools that speed up sketching and make it possible to construct more complex, realistic shapes.

  • Offset, Trim, Extend
  • Mirror & Pattern
  • Complex sketches
📘 What I Learned

I learned how tools like offset, trim, extend, mirror and pattern speed up sketching and make it possible to build more complex, realistic shapes.

DAY04
Core Day 🚀
3D Modeling
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 4

Day 4 is the core of the module — turning flat sketches into real 3D geometry with Extrude and Revolve, then refining the edges with Fillet and Chamfer.

  • Convert sketch → 3D using Extrude
  • Convert sketch → 3D using Revolve
  • Modify tools: Fillet
  • Modify tools: Chamfer
📘 What I Learned

I learned how to turn flat sketches into real 3D geometry using Extrude and Revolve, and how to refine edges cleanly with Fillet and Chamfer.

DAY05
Assemblies
Assembly & Motion
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 5

Day 5 moves from single parts to assemblies, looking at how components relate to each other and how joints bring motion into a design.

  • Components vs Bodies
  • Joint tool
  • Motion study
📘 What I Learned

I learned the difference between components and bodies, and how the Joint tool brings realistic motion into an assembly.

DAY06
Application
Engineering Practice
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 6

Day 6 applies everything learned so far to practical engineering parts, along with the tools used to check dimensions and accuracy.

  • Create real parts: Spur gear (basic)
  • Create real parts: Plate with holes
  • Learn: Measure tool
  • Learn: Inspect
📘 What I Learned

I learned to apply modeling skills to practical engineering parts like gears and plates, and how to use the Measure and Inspect tools to check accuracy.

DAY07
Final Project 🎯
Final Project
+
🖼️ Add a photo for Day 7

Day 7 brings the whole week together in one final project, applying sketching, modeling, and assembly skills to a complete design.

  • Combine everything learned across the week
📘 What I Learned

I learned how to bring sketching, modeling and assembly skills together into a single, complete final design.
